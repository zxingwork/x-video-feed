「X 视频导出器」隐私权政策

最后更新:2026 年 9 月 20 日 · 适用版本:1.1.0 及以后

「X 视频导出器」(下称「本扩展」)是一个 Chrome 浏览器扩展,只有一个用途:把你正在浏览的 x.com 页面上已经加载的视频推文整理成清单,并把视频文件下载到你自己电脑上的文件夹。本政策说明本扩展处理哪些数据、如何使用、是否共享。

一句话总结:所有数据都在你自己的电脑上处理。本扩展不向开发者或任何第三方服务器发送任何数据,不收集个人信息,没有统计、没有广告、不读取你的登录凭据或 cookie。

1. 本扩展处理哪些数据

仅当你主动点击扩展图标并使用其功能时,本扩展才会处理下列数据:

  • 你当前打开的 x.com 页面上的内容:该页面已经加载的推文——推文链接、作者用户名、发布时间、正文片段,以及其中视频文件的 URL 与分辨率。这些内容由你已登录的浏览器会话从 x.com 取得,本扩展只是读取页面已经拿到的数据。
  • 你当前标签页的网址:仅用于判断当前页面是不是 x.com,以决定是否启用「扫描」按钮。不记录、不留存。
  • 你选择的本地保存文件夹:由你通过浏览器的文件夹选择框亲自授权,本扩展只向该文件夹写入视频文件与清单文件。

本扩展不处理:你的 X 账号密码或其他登录凭据、cookie、支付与财务信息、健康信息、精确位置、通讯录,也不读取 x.com 以外任何网站的内容。

2. 数据如何使用与存放

  • 收集到的清单保存在浏览器的会话存储(chrome.storage.session)与内存中,用于在你点击扩展图标时显示计数、以及在你点击下载时知道要下载哪些文件。关闭浏览器后该数据即被清除。
  • 下载的视频文件与导出的 CSV / JSON 清单文件,写在你自己选择的本地文件夹里。除了你自己,任何一方都无法访问。
  • 本扩展没有任何服务器端组件,不做统计上报、不做行为分析、不做广告投放。

3. 数据是否被传输或共享

  • 不向开发者或任何第三方传输任何数据。本扩展没有自建服务器,不使用远程代码,不接入任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。
  • 下载视频时,本扩展向 X 的视频 CDN video.twimg.com 发起 HTTPS 的 GET / HEAD 请求以获取视频文件本身,这是唯一的外部网络请求,由你点击「开始下载」触发。
  • 导出的清单文件仅保存在你的电脑上,是否分享给你自己决定。

4. 数据出售与用途限定

  • 不出售、不出租、不转让用户数据给任何第三方。
  • 不将用户数据用于与上述单一用途无关的目的。
  • 不将用户数据用于判定信用度或用于放贷。
  • 不使用用户数据做广告定向或任何形式的变现。

5. 权限与对应用途

  • storage — 在会话存储中保存扫描结果,供下载与导出使用。
  • scripting — 在你点击扩展后,向你当前所在的 x.com 标签页注入只读脚本,读取该页面已加载推文的媒体信息。
  • activeTab — 仅在你点击扩展图标时访问当前标签页。
  • downloads — 提供备用的「用浏览器下载」通道,以及导出 CSV / JSON 清单文件。
  • https://x.com/*、https://twitter.com/* — 在该站点页面注入只读扫描脚本。
  • https://video.twimg.com/* — 你点击下载后,从该 CDN 获取视频文件。

6. 安全性

所有外部通信均使用 HTTPS。本扩展不写入、不修改 x.com 页面内容,不代你点赞、发帖、转发或关注,也不绕过任何技术保护措施。

7. 儿童

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童,也不会有意收集其任何数据。

8. 政策变更

本政策如有变更,会在本页更新并修改顶部的「最后更新」日期。涉及数据处理的重大变更会在扩展的更新说明中一并告知。

9. 联系方式

对本政策或数据处理有任何疑问,请联系:github.com/zxingwork/xvid-ext-privacy/issues

Privacy Policy — X Video Exporter

Last updated: September 20, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0 and later

X Video Exporter (the "Extension") is a Chrome extension with a single purpose: to build a list of the video posts already loaded on the x.com page you are viewing, and download those videos to a folder on your own computer. This policy explains what data the Extension handles, how it is used, and whether it is shared.

In one sentence: everything is processed on your own device. The Extension sends no data to the developer or to any third-party server, collects no personal information, and contains no analytics, no ads, and no remote code.

1. Data the Extension handles

Only when you actively click the Extension icon and use its features:

  • Content of the x.com page you have open: posts the page has already loaded — post URLs, author usernames, timestamps, text snippets, and the URLs and resolutions of their video files. This content is fetched by your own logged-in browser session; the Extension only reads data the page already has.
  • The URL of your current tab: used solely to check whether the current page is x.com, so the scan button can be enabled. It is neither stored nor logged.
  • The local folder you choose: authorized by you through the browser's folder picker. The Extension only writes video and index files into that folder.

The Extension does not handle your X password or other credentials, cookies, financial or payment information, health information, precise location, or contacts, and does not read content from any site other than x.com.

2. How data is used and stored

  • The collected list is kept in the browser's session storage (chrome.storage.session) and in memory, so the Extension can show counts and know what to download when you click. It is cleared when the browser is closed.
  • Downloaded videos and exported CSV/JSON index files are written to the local folder you picked. No one else can access them.
  • The Extension has no server-side component: no telemetry, no behavioural analytics, no advertising.

3. Transmission and sharing

  • No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. No backend, no remote code, no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
  • When downloading, the Extension makes HTTPS GET / HEAD requests to X's video CDN video.twimg.com to fetch the video files. This is the only external request, triggered by you clicking "start download".
  • Exported index files stay on your computer; sharing them is your choice.

4. Sale of data and purpose limitation

  • User data is not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party.
  • User data is not used for purposes unrelated to the single purpose described above.
  • User data is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • User data is not used for ad targeting or any form of monetisation.

5. Permissions and their purposes

  • storage — store scan results in session storage for download and export.
  • scripting — after you click the Extension, inject read-only scripts into your current x.com tab to read media info from posts the page has loaded.
  • activeTab — access the current tab only when you click the Extension icon.
  • downloads — provide the optional browser-download path and export CSV/JSON index files.
  • https://x.com/*, https://twitter.com/* — inject the read-only scanning script on those sites.
  • https://video.twimg.com/* — fetch video files from that CDN after you click download.

6. Security

All external communication uses HTTPS. The Extension does not write to or modify x.com pages, does not like, post, repost or follow on your behalf, and does not circumvent any technical protection measure.

7. Children

The Extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date above. Material changes will also be noted in the Extension's update notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handling: github.com/zxingwork/xvid-ext-privacy/issues
